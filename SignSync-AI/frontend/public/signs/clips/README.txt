SIGN VIDEOS (optional)
======================
Put one short video per sign here, with the sign's lowercase name:
  alphabet videos:  public/signs/alphabet/a.mp4 ... z.mp4
  word videos:      public/signs/words/hello.mp4, water.mp4, ...   (names in src/signs.js)

The app plays a video first. If there is no video, it shows the photo, then the picture card.
Use videos you recorded yourself, or videos whose licence allows this use. Keep each video short (2 to 4 seconds)
and small (under 2 MB) so the page loads quickly.
