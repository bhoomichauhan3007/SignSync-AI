VIDEO DICTIONARY
================
Each video here is one word, named with its lowercase slug, for example:  thank_you.mp4, hospital.mp4, market.mp4

These videos are NOT in the project ZIP. Add them on your own computer:
  python scripts\install_sign_videos.py --zip "<path to ISL_40_Words.zip>"

The source videos have different licences. Read CREDITS.txt (created by the script) and keep the videos for
research or education use within the team unless the licence of each video allows sharing.
