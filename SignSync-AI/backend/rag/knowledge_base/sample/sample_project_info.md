## About SignSync AI

SignSync AI is a college major project that connects sign language with speech and text. It combines
computer vision for hand tracking, a sign recognition model, natural language processing, a backend
API, a web frontend and an AI tutor. The AI tutor answers questions from documents in the knowledge
base and says clearly when the knowledge base does not contain an answer.

## About Indian Sign Language

Indian Sign Language (ISL) is the sign language used by many deaf people in India. It is a separate
language from American Sign Language and British Sign Language. In general sign-language linguistics,
a sign is described by its handshape, the orientation of the palm, its location relative to the body,
its movement, and non-manual features such as facial expression. Verified ISL dictionary entries must
come from a qualified source and are not contained in this sample file.

## General learning advice (general guidance, not verified ISL content)

Learners often begin with the manual alphabet and numbers, then move to greetings and everyday words.
Practising in front of a mirror and learning with a certified ISL teacher are common suggestions.
Short, regular practice sessions are usually more effective than long, infrequent ones.

## Emergency information

In India, the single national emergency number is 112. In a real emergency, contact emergency services
directly and do not rely on this application.
